# Gemini concise random K-pop dance prompt

Use the provided character reference as the visual source and first frame. The sole subject is the same clearly adult fictional female VTuber. Preserve her identity, face, short hairstyle, hair color, eyes, headwear, outfit, accessories, body proportions, colors, and anime art style throughout.

Create one continuous, silent, approximately 10-second, full-body K-pop dance shot with mature, sensual, confident, playful stage energy. Design fresh choreography for each generation rather than following a fixed sequence. Select 3–4 primary movement phrases from this pool: runway-style cross-step, controlled hip roll, body roll, torso wave, shoulder hit, strong weight shift, low crouch with controlled rise, pivot turn, smooth spin, or a brief back-facing hip movement with an over-the-shoulder glance. Connect them with natural steps and weight transfers. Prioritize strong beat accents, clear level or direction changes, and complete movement arcs over many small actions.

Keep the motion physically believable and fluid. Feet establish balance before weight shifts; hips lead the torso; shoulders, arms, hair, and clothing follow with subtle inertia and rebound. Expressions change naturally between confident eye contact, an intense gaze, and a subtle seductive smile. Avoid static posing, repetitive swaying, frantic twitching, or pose-to-pose snapping.

Use a completely fixed, eye-level camera and unchanged focal length. Keep the character centered and fully visible from head to toe with safe space around all limbs during every step, turn, lean, and crouch. No camera movement or reframing.

The background is a perfectly flat, uniform chroma-key green layer, RGB `#00FF00`, with no physical room, floor, horizon, texture, gradient, lighting variation, reflection, glow, halo, ambient occlusion, or any cast/contact/drop shadow, including beneath the feet.

Plan the loop from the beginning. During the final 1–1.5 seconds, use natural choreography to return smoothly to the opening pose, foot placement, hand position, head angle, expression, body orientation, scale, framing, and screen position. The ending should flow directly into the opening without a snap.
